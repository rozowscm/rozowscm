### Casey Rozowski

Assistant Professor of Economics, University of Wisconsin–Eau Claire

I am an applied microeconomist working in environmental, energy, and public economics. My research uses causal inference and large administrative and environmental datasets to study how pollution controls affect air quality and mortality, how ownership changes affect the performance of wind and solar plants, and how financial-aid and admissions policies shape college enrollment. I also develop Bayesian methods for learning and decision-making under structural uncertainty.
